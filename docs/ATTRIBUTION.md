# Provenance and attribution

FrameShrink application code was prepared on 6 November 2026 with AI assistance. Existing planning documents dated 27 October 2026 are preserved separately in `../output/returnrail-frameshrink/`; they predate this implementation.

The build reuses the MIT-licensed [workshop sidecar](https://github.com/forshtat/mempool-workshop-sidecar/tree/2e22ff0a2898e69ea4bf5275bce1be8581a8582a), originally derived from Etherspot Skandha. Its original copyright and license are preserved in the dependency checkout and `UPSTREAM-LICENSE.txt`.

Directly reused upstream components are FrameTx types, RLP codec/signature hash, protocol signature validation, prefix recognition and structural checks, the trace policy validator and CheckCollector. Upstream negative fixtures informed the gas-budget and banned-opcode control families. The APPROVE runtimes use the same documented protocol pattern. These mechanisms are upstream work, not new FrameShrink inventions.

New FrameShrink work includes typed recipe/ABI construction, deterministic reduction, stable-frame mapping, controlled runtime profile, state/permission gates, bundle/checksum/replay behavior, local UI and CLI, verification, separate sponsor-proof automation and documentation. The owned `PayloadSink.sol` provides an ABI-valid array-edit dimension. The pinned ethrex image and inherited genesis fixture come from the upstream runtime; the three owned genesis entries are explicitly generated by `scripts/fixtures.ts`.

This provenance does not establish hackathon eligibility. Preserve creation dates and disclose advance research and AI assistance in any submission. Obtain the organizer's applicable track ruling before making eligibility claims. No prize or eligibility guarantee is made.

The unified CLI addition implements an owner-bound conditional-TIMESTAMP validator, scoped deployment/signing, public/local case orchestration, captured account reconstruction, signer-free replay, checkpointed submissions and terminal formatting. SIGPARAM operand semantics were checked against the pinned ethrex `docs/eip-8141.md`. The validator is new test-fixture bytecode, while its protocol operations and sidecar checks remain upstream work.
